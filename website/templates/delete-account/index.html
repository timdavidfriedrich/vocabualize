Delete your account

In the app (fastest)

  • Signed in with Google: open Settings → Delete account, type the confirmation word and confirm. Your account and all synced data are deleted right away.
  • Without an account: open Settings → Delete all data. Everything is removed from your phone, and your install key is deleted from our server.

Without the app

Send an email to {{owner.email}} from the email address of your Google account, with the subject “Delete my Vocabualize account”. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • your account: the link to your Google account, your name, email address and profile picture,
  • all words, collections, learning progress, activity days and settings,
  • all your photos on our server,
  • your usage counters for translations, photo searches and scans.

What is kept

  • Feedback and reports you sent stay without any link to your account. They contain no name or email address and are deleted one year after they were sent.
  • Technical server logs are deleted automatically, see the privacy policy, section 10.
  • Data on your phones stays there until you delete it with Delete all data or uninstall the app.
