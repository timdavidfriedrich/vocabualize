Vocabualize helps you learn the words you actually need: the ones around you.

ADD WORDS YOUR WAY
• Type a word, or scan an object with your camera and pick a suggestion.
• Get a translation and a matching photo automatically, or take your own photo.
• Sort words into collections.

REMEMBER THEM
• Practice with spaced repetition: every word comes back right before you would forget it.
• Rate how well you knew it, or type the answer.
• Listen to the pronunciation.

STAY MOTIVATED
• Build a streak. Freezes save it on a day you miss.
• Optional reminders to practice or to keep your streak.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• Works offline and without an account.
• Sign in with Google only if you want to sync between devices.
• Export a backup at any time.
• No ads, no tracking.
